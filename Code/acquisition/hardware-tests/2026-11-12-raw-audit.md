threshold 1553.3, and hold 0.5 s. Calibration explicitly warned that no grid setting
reached 90% recall. The selected highest-recall fit gave training precision 50.0%,
recall 80.0%, and 10.0 natural false events/minute.

On separate 114212-relabelled data it gave 16/40 hits, precision 61.5%, recall
40.0%, and 2.47 natural false events/minute. On 122332 it detected no events.
Neither passes the 90% precision/recall and at-most-one natural false event/minute
targets. The fit was deliberately not distributed as an approved/default model.
